How the F1 Fantasy Draft Works

Draft format: 4 players, 8 rounds each (snake draft). Each pick gets a multiplier that increases with later rounds: R1=1x, R2=1.5x, R3=2x, R4=3x, R5=4x, R6=5x, R7=6x, R8=8x.

Driver picks: Score the driver's actual F1 championship points, multiplied by the pick's multiplier.

Team picks: Score the average of the two drivers' points for that constructor (constructor championship points ÷ 2), multiplied by the pick's multiplier.

Winning: Highest total weighted points at end of season wins.

The catch: Later-round picks have huge multipliers, so a savvy mid-tier pick at 8x can outscore a superstar at 1x. The key strategic tension is whether to lock in guaranteed high-floor picks early or gamble on breakout candidates in the high-multiplier late rounds.
