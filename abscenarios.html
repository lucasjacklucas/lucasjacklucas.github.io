What would it take to win NO on the Nine?

Say you wanted Albertans to vote NO on the nine ballot questions — the immigration and constitutional ones — as part of a campaign to keep Alberta in Canada (a Remain vote on separation). What would it actually take?

For each question, this tool shows two routes to a NO, and exactly how much of each you'd need: persuade some Remain supporters to vote No on that question, or turn out more Remain voters than the other side. When a route simply can't work for a question, it says so and explains why.
