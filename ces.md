# Purpose of the Study

The purpose of this study is to better understand the experiences and perspectives of individuals who are involved in the Calgary 2025 election, including candidates, volunteers, and campaign officials. More specifically, I am interested in understanding how candidates and political parties build their teams and come to understand their supporters, how individuals involved in the municipal political process perceive political parties, and how individuals involved in municipal campaigns think about voters. 

# What Type of Personal Information Will be Collected? 

I will collect information on the demographic identities (e.g. gender, age group, racial identity) of some participants if that information is disclosed as part of naturally occurring conversations. I will never disclose personal information if it would risk making a participant personally identifiable.
 
# Are There Risks or Benefits if I Participate? 
 
There are no known risks to participating in this study, nor are there any direct benefits to you for participating. I hope that this research will improve our understanding of municipal policy and politics. 
 
# What Happens to the Information I Provide? 
 
I am deeply committed to keeping your data confidential. My field notes will be securely stored in a password-protected and encrypted folder, and only one individual (Jack Lucas) will ever have access to this information. Your participation is voluntary, and you have the right to refuse to participate or to withdraw without negative consequences. You are free to withdraw from the study until November 1, 2025. To withdraw from the study, please contact me (jack.lucas@ucalgary.ca) to communicate that you wish to withdraw, along with a reminder of where and when we interacted, so that I can remove our interaction from my field notes. 

The information that you provide will be used in academic research on municipal elections, democracy, and representation. To protect confidentiality and privacy, your information will be protected in the following ways: 

- I will never record your personal name, even in my hand-written field notes. 
- I will never disclose the location of our interaction at a level below that of the Community. 
- I will never disclose personal information about you if there is any risk that such information could allow another person to identify you. For instance, I would only disclose your gender or age group if our interaction took place in a context involving many others of the same gender or age group.
